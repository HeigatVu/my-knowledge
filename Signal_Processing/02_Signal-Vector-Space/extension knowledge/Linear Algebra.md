# Linear Algebra in Signal Processing

### Context & Signal Type
* **Type of signal:** Finite-length or Periodic
* **Characteristic:** Lists of $N$ real or complex numbers. Commonly used for [[Discrete Fourier Transforms]] (DFT).

---

# Coordinate Spaces

## 1. $\mathbb{R}^N$ ($n$-dimensional Euclidean Space)
* **Definition:** The generalization of coordinate space to $n$ dimensions. Vectors are ordered lists of $n$ real numbers:
  $$\mathbf{x} = (x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n)$$
* **Note on Visualization:** We cannot visualize this space directly for $n > 3$, but the mathematical properties remain analogous to 2D and 3D spaces.

## 2. $\mathbb{C}^N$ ($n$-dimensional Complex Space)
* **Definition:** A vector space where the individual components are complex numbers:
  $$\mathbf{z} = (z_1, z_2, \dots, z_n)$$
* **Component Form:** Each component $z_i$ is of the form:
  $$z_i = a + bi$$
  Where $a$ and $b$ are real numbers ($\mathbb{R}$), and $i$ is the imaginary unit ($i = \sqrt{-1}$).
