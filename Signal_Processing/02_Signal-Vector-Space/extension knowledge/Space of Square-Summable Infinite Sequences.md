# Space of Square-Summable Infinite Sequences ($\ell_2(\mathbb{Z})$)

* **Type of Signal:** Infinite discrete-time signal.
* **Core Concept:** A space consisting of infinite sequences where the elements don't "blow up" to infinity, ensuring the signal possesses **finite energy**.

> [!MATH] Mathematical Definition
> The set of all infinite sequences of real (or complex) numbers:
> $$\mathbf{x} = (\dots, x_{-2}, x_{-1}, x_0, x_1, x_2, \dots)$$
> bound by the constraint that the sum of the absolute squares is finite:
> $$\sum_{i=-\infty}^{\infty} |x_i|^2 < \infty$$

* **Application:** Crucial for analyzing stable, infinite-duration discrete systems and digital signal filters.
