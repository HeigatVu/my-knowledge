# Space of Square-Integrable Functions ($L_2([a, b])$)

* **Type of Signal:** Continuous-time signal.
* **Core Concept:** A space where functions behave like vectors, and their "coordinates" are completely continuous (like a continuous sine wave). 

> [!MATH] Mathematical Definition
> The set of all real (or complex)-valued functions $f(t)$ defined over a closed interval $[a, b]$, such that the integral of the square of the absolute value is finite:
> $$\int_{a}^{b} |f(t)|^2 \, dt < \infty$$

* **Application:** This finite-integral condition mathematically ensures that the continuous signal has **finite energy**. It serves as the foundation for Fourier series expansions and quantum mechanical wavefunctions.
